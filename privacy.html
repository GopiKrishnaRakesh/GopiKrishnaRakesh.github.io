Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers Python Reels Poster (“the app”), the private publishing tool used by the LearnPython2027 channel. The app is operated by the owner of LearnPython2027 and is used only to publish that channel's own videos.

Who uses the app

Only the channel owner signs in to the app. It is not offered to the public, and no other person can create an account.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app receives permission (OAuth tokens) for the owner's own YouTube channel, and uses it to:

  • upload the owner's own videos, with their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and scheduled publish times;
  • create and add videos to the owner's own playlists;
  • read basic channel details (channel name, ID and picture) to show which channel is connected;
  • read view and engagement statistics for the owner's own videos (YouTube Analytics API).

The app does not access, collect or store personal data about viewers or any other YouTube user.

How the data is stored

OAuth tokens and scheduling data are stored only in the app's database on the channel owner's own computer. They are not sent to or shared with any third party, other than Google itself when the app calls the YouTube APIs.

Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked or the channel is disconnected from the app, the stored tokens are deleted from the app's database. Stored data is also deleted within 30 days of a deletion request sent to the contact address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
